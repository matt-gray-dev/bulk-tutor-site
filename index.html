Which apps work?

ManaBox, Moxfield, more

Every app below brings your cards and how many you have. Only some bring where they're kept.

ManaBoxbinders become locations

In the Collection tab, tap the menu at the top right and export the collection as a CSV. To send one binder, open it and use its own menu.

Binder names become locations, deck binders become decks, and your printing's picture comes along. With Dropbox connected, save the export into Dropbox › Apps › Bulk Tutor and it's picked up the next time Bulk Tutor syncs.

Dragon Shieldfolders become locations

In the Dragon Shield app, export your collection as a CSV.

Folder names become locations. Folders whose names start with your deck prefix (see Settings below) count as decks.

Moxfieldone location

On moxfield.com, open your collection and choose Export.

Moxfield's export has no binders, so every card goes in one location called Collection.

Archidektone location

On archidekt.com, open your collection, choose Export and download the CSV.

All cards go in one location; your printing's picture comes along.

Deckboxone location

On deckbox.org, open your inventory and export it as a CSV.

All cards go in one location.

Other appsmost CSV exports

TCGplayer, MTGGoldfish, Cardsphere, Helvault, Deckstats and most other apps work too: any CSV with a card name column and a quantity column. A column called Location, Binder, Folder or List becomes locations.

Anything elsepaste a list

Copy a text list, one card per line (“1 Sol Ring”), and paste it in the box above.

Settings

ManaBox exports also mark deck binders on their own, so this is only a backup. For any other box or binder that holds a deck, use “Choose your decks” on the Collection page.

Collection link

A link with your collection packed into it. Bookmark it, add it to your home screen, or open it on another device. It's a snapshot, so copy a new one after you re-import.
